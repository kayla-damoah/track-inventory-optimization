Recommended plan

1

Order once, in September

Consolidate purchasing into a single bulk order before the season builds, to earn the volume discount.

2

Stock by forecasted size

Order from the roster's size mix plus a 10% buffer instead of equal quantities of every size.

3

Replace on a schedule

Competition spikes wear out in 6 to 8 months. Track issue dates and keep buffer stock so replacements are planned, not rushed.
